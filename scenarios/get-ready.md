# Get Ready for a Meeting

## Situation and goal

You are speaking to a colleague before a meeting. You need another minute and have not checked the report yet. Your goal is to explain your status and promise a specific response time.

{% assign lesson = site.pages | where: 'lesson_id', page.lesson_id_ref | first %}
[Open the lesson]({{ lesson.url | relative_url }}).

## Your turn

Answer each partner turn in one or two sentences. Use a hint only if you need it. Your answers are not recorded.

{% include roleplay.html %}

## Change the situation and switch roles

**Change:** You do not know which report they mean. Ask “Do you mean the report for today's meeting?” before checking.

**Switch roles:** Ask about readiness, check understanding, and request a response time. Your partner should answer one turn at a time.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Model dialogue — read after your attempt</summary>

**Colleague:** Are you ready for the meeting?  
**You:** Not yet. I'm getting ready. Give me one minute.

**Colleague:** Do you understand what we need to discuss?  
**You:** Yes, I get it now. We need to discuss the deadline.

**Colleague:** Can you confirm whether the report is ready?  
**You:** Let me check. I'll get back to you soon.

**Colleague:** Can you get back to me before ten?  
**You:** Yes. I'll get back to you by nine fifty.

</details>

## Practice with an AI partner

Copy the prompt below into your preferred AI chat. The website itself does not connect to an AI service.

```text
Play my colleague before a meeting. I am the learner, and I need another minute to get ready. I have not checked whether the report is ready. My goal is to explain my status and promise a response time.
My level is A2–B1. Use short, natural English and stay in your assigned role.
Give only one short turn, then wait for my reply. Respond to what I actually say.
Do not give my lines, reveal the full dialogue, or decide what I have agreed to.
Introduce this change later: After I offer to check, make the report ambiguous: ask whether I checked the report without naming it. Let me ask which report you mean before continuing.
If I ask "What should I say next?", give one concise hint, then wait again.
Save corrections until I say "End this session", unless an error blocks meaning.
At the end, use: You said -> Better -> Why -> One reusable example.
Label corrections as Must fix, Natural upgrade, or Optional style.
Base feedback on my actual replies. Do not assess listening or pronunciation from text alone.
Start with this one partner turn: "Are you ready for the meeting?"
```
