# Get Ready for a Meeting

## Situation and goal

You are speaking to a colleague before a meeting. You need another minute and have not checked the report yet. Your goal is to explain your status and promise a specific response time.

## Your turn

Answer each partner turn in one or two sentences. Use a hint only if you need it. Your answers are not recorded.

{% include roleplay.html %}

## Change the situation and switch roles

**Change:** You do not know which report they mean. Ask “Do you mean the report for today's meeting?” before checking.

**Switch roles:** Ask about readiness, check understanding, and request a response time. Your partner should answer one turn at a time.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Model dialogue — read after your attempt</summary>

**Colleague:** Are you ready for the meeting?  
**You:** Not yet. I'm getting ready. Give me one minute.

**Colleague:** Do you understand what we need to discuss?  
**You:** Yes, I get it now. We need to discuss the deadline.

**Colleague:** Can you confirm whether the report is ready?  
**You:** Let me check. I'll get back to you soon.

**Colleague:** Can you get back to me before ten?  
**You:** Yes. I'll get back to you by nine fifty.

</details>
