# Share a Guesthouse Lounge

## Situation and goal

You and a friend are staying at a guesthouse, a small place for travelers. You are sitting in its shared lounge. You feel cold and want to close the window yourself. Your phone needs charging.

Your friend has a charger and a spare cable. You have your own charger in your bag and a spare pen they can borrow. Ask before using anything. Respect a refusal and ask about another option.

## Your turn

For the first turn, ask permission to close the window. Say each reply before continuing. Keep the model closed until you have tried.

These website turns follow a fixed path. They do not understand, adapt to, or grade your replies. Use the AI prompt below for a conversation that responds to what you say.

## Change the situation and switch roles {#change-situation}

**Change:** The spare cable does not fit your phone. Your own charger is in your bag. Ask permission to use the socket by the door with it. A socket is where you plug in a charger. Wait for permission before acting.

**Switch roles:** Be the friend. Allow the window to be closed. You are using your charger, so refuse that request and offer the spare cable. Ask to borrow the other person's pen. Give one turn, then wait for each reply.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Sample responses for the change and role switch</summary>

**Change — sample:** “The cable doesn't fit my phone. Is it okay if I use the socket by the door with my own charger?” After permission: “Thanks.” After a refusal: “That's okay. I'll charge it later.”

**Role switch — possible turns:**

1. After the window request: “Sure, go ahead.”
2. After the charger request: “Sorry, I'm using it right now. You can use my spare cable.”
3. After a request for the cable: “Sure, go ahead. It's on the table.”
4. Ask “Could I borrow your pen?” and wait. If they agree, thank them. If they refuse, respect that answer.

Use only the turns that fit your partner's actual requests. Success means giving a clear decision, explaining the real limit, and waiting for permission yourself.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Model dialogue — read after your attempt</summary>

**Friend:** Hi. Are you comfortable here?  
**You:** I'm a little cold. Could I close the window, please?

**Friend:** Sure, go ahead. Do you need to charge your phone too?  
**You:** Yes. Could I use your charger, please?

**Friend:** Sorry, I'm using my charger right now. I have a spare cable, though.  
**You:** Is it okay if I use your spare cable instead?

**Friend:** Sure, go ahead. The cable is on the table.  
**You:** Thanks. I'll try the cable.

**Friend:** Could I borrow your pen while you do that?  
**You:** Sure, go ahead.

</details>

Other natural replies are valid. Check that you name the action, wait for permission, respect the charger refusal, and respond to the pen request.
